Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what clients ask most.

How long does an app take to build?

A focused app typically takes 2–6 weeks depending on scope. We share a clear milestone plan before we start and send weekly progress updates.

Do I own the source code?

Yes — you own the complete source code and all assets on delivery. We hand over the repository and documentation.

Can you publish on my accounts?

Yes. We publish to Google Play and the Apple App Store on your developer accounts and handle store listings, compliance and review.

What does it cost?

Fixed packages start from $25 for publishing and $95–$120 for app builds. Larger or custom work gets a scoped quote — see Services & pricing.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer post-launch support and maintenance so your product keeps working as platforms and dependencies change. Write to support@ryzadev.com and we’ll pick it up.

How do we communicate and get updates?

Over email, WhatsApp or your preferred channel — whichever is fastest for you. You'll get regular updates and can see working builds as we go.
