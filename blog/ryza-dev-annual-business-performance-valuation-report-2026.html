The business in 2026

Ryza Dev is a registered software company in Gobindaganj, Gaibandha, Bangladesh, operating under Trade License 000491 as a sole proprietorship owned by Md Zubair Alam Roktim. The studio runs two complementary revenue engines:

  • Client services — Android and Flutter app development, AI integration, web platforms, publishing, automation and cloud work.
  • Owned products — a growing catalogue of apps published on Google Play, including utility, media and networking products.

Services fund the studio and produce predictable cash flow; owned products compound in value as installs, retention and monetisation accumulate. The valuation reflects both.

How the valuation was built

The estimate uses a deliberately conservative, three-part method. It is a management estimate, not an appraisal, and we say so everywhere the number appears.

1. Service business

Annualised revenue from client work, capitalised at a low single-digit multiple. A founder-led studio in an emerging market does not command the multiples of a venture-backed software company, and the model does not pretend otherwise.

2. Product portfolio

The published Google Play catalogue is valued on install base, retention and monetisation potential — not raw download counts, which are easy to inflate and hard to monetise.

3. Assets and intellectual property

Proprietary codebases, reusable internal tooling, the brand, domains, store accounts and verified business credentials. For a studio, this is often the most durable part of the value.

What the growth projection assumes

The path to $75,000 by the next fiscal cycle assumes three things happen together: the published app portfolio keeps adding titles with real retention, automation retainers convert into recurring revenue, and the studio adds higher-value product engagements instead of competing on the lowest price. If only one of those holds, the figure lands nearer the current midpoint than the projection.
