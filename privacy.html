Privacy Policy

How Ryza Dev collects, uses and protects personal data.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RYZA DEV (“Ryza Dev”, “we”, “us”), a software company registered in Bangladesh under Trade Licence 000491 and owned by Md Zubair Alam Roktim, handles personal data in connection with our website at ryzadev.com, our client engagements, and the mobile applications we develop and publish.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Name, email address, phone number and company name when you contact us or request a quote.
  • Project details, requirements and any files or credentials you share for a project.
  • Billing and payment information needed to invoice for services.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard server logs: IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and timestamps.
  • Aggregate analytics about how visitors use the website.

Information from our applications

For apps we publish, the data collected is described in that app’s own privacy policy and Google Play Data Safety declaration. Where an app displays advertising, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process a device advertising identifier and approximate location to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent choices.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries, prepare proposals and deliver contracted work.
  • To operate, maintain and secure our website and applications.
  • To issue invoices and meet accounting and tax obligations.
  • To comply with legal requirements and enforce our terms.

3. Legal bases

We process personal data where it is necessary to perform a contract with you, where we have your consent, where we have a legitimate interest in running and improving our business, or where we are required to do so by law.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us operate (for example hosting, email, analytics, advertising and payment providers), with professional advisers, or where required by law. Client project data is shared only with team members working on that project.

5. Retention

We keep enquiry and project records for as long as needed to deliver the work and to meet legal, accounting and tax requirements. Server logs are retained for a limited period and then deleted or anonymised.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted transport (HTTPS), access controls and least-privilege access to client systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you, and you may object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, email hello@ryzadev.com. We will respond within a reasonable period.

8. Cookies

The website uses only the storage needed to function and, where enabled, privacy-respecting analytics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may stop working as a result.

9. Children

Our services are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Some service providers process data outside Bangladesh. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards and provider terms that protect the data.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows the current version.
