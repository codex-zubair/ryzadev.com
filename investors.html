How the estimate is calculated

The number is built from three components, each deliberately conservative:

  • Service business. Annualised revenue from app development, publishing and automation work, capitalised at a low single-digit multiple appropriate to a founder-led studio in an emerging market.
  • Product portfolio. The published Google Play catalogue, estimated on install base, retention and monetisation potential rather than download counts alone.
  • Assets and IP. Proprietary codebases, reusable internal tooling, the brand, domains, store accounts and verified business credentials.

The disclosed range reflects uncertainty in forward revenue: the low end assumes service revenue holds at its current level, the high end assumes the app portfolio and automation retainers scale as planned.
